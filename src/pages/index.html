WHAT WE MANUFACTURE

Our Product Range

Manufacturer based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, GST-registered since 2017 — from a single custom piece to a bulk order, every batch runs through the same machines and checks.

Brass Bush

Brass bush, brass metal bush and brass roller bush in standard sizes including 1 inch bore.

Nuts

Hex nuts and automotive nuts in mild steel, priced and supplied per piece or in bulk.

Bolts

Machine nut bolts, bolt caps and hinge bolts machined and threaded to standard sizes.

Brass Nut

Brass moulding nut and mould nut, machined for die-mould and moulding industry fitments.

Metal Bush

Metal bush and automotive bush turned to size for machinery and vehicle applications.

Metal Bolt

Brass nut bolt, stainless steel bolts and automotive bolts for higher-grade fitments.

Nut Bolt

Combined nut-bolt sets and plastic nuts, sold per piece or per kg for bulk hardware orders.

Industrial Fasteners

Wifi fibre nuts, die mould components and pipe fittings for industrial and telecom use.
